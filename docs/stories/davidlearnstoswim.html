David Learns To Swim
Once upon a time, there was a duck named David. All his life David wanted to learn how to swim. He decided to enroll in a swim class.

When he got to the swim class, the teacher asked, “Does everyone know how to do kung fu?" Everyone said yes, except for David.

The teacher told him that he needed to know kung fu before he could join the swim class. David sadly left the waiting area, and went next door to enroll in the kung fu class.

5 years later the kung fu teacher said, “Now you know kung fu! Now you can go to swim class, David!"

David excitedly went to swim class the next day. He was so excited he didn’t even wait and jumped right in.

When he jumped in the pool, he didn’t sink. In fact, he was already swimming!

He swam and swam and swam until he got too tired to swim anymore. At that point, he went home to his pond, and he went to sleep.

The end.



The moral of the story is:
Learning kung fu can always be helpful even if you don’t really need it.
